# Ilustración de aplicaciones

## Composición actual: imágenes independientes

La diapositiva «Algunas aplicaciones» utiliza ahora dos figuras separadas. Cada imagen queda directamente encima de su explicación, sin depender de alinear dos textos bajo una imagen compuesta.

- `public/assets/aplicacion-sistemas-complejos.png`: estructuras conceptuales para hablar de simular sistemas complejos y estudiar cómo cambian sus propiedades.
- `public/assets/aplicacion-rutas.png`: mapa conceptual para hablar de buscar una ruta corta que visite varios puntos.

Ambas se crearon con la herramienta integrada ImageGen el 15 de octubre de 2026. Las ilustraciones no son modelos científicos validados, rutas calculadas ni evidencia de ventaja cuántica. La imagen compuesta original se conserva como referencia.

### Prompt de sistemas complejos

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create ONE standalone editorial 3D illustration for the chemistry/materials half of a clean academic slide. Wide 3:2 framing, pure white background. Show a complex molecular material made of many connected atoms, in two clearly distinct nearby configurations of the same material, one more open and one denser. Use restrained teal spheres, neutral grey connecting bonds, realistic soft shadows, crisp professional scientific illustration. The visual should suggest simulating a complex system and studying how changes in its structure affect its properties. No battery, no city, no map, no text, no labels, no arrows, no graphs, no claims or branding. Keep the entire subject centered and contained with generous white margins. This is a conceptual illustration, not a validated molecular model.

### Prompt de rutas

> Use case: productivity-visual. Create ONE standalone editorial 3D illustration for the optimization half of a clean academic slide. Wide 3:2 framing, pure white background. Show a simple isometric street map viewed from above with six clearly visible location pins at different intersections. Show a few thin neutral grey candidate routes and one prominent violet #6366f1 route connecting the locations to suggest looking for a short route to visit several places. Use a few low neutral grey buildings for context, minimal visual clutter, subtle teal accents and soft shadows. No chemistry, no molecule, no text, no letters, no numbers, no arrows, no metrics, no checkmarks, no branding. The image illustrates a route-planning research problem, not a proven quantum solution. Center and contain the map with generous white margins.
